Bibi Ka Maqbara

📍
2.4 km from your location
🏛

About this place

Bibi Ka Maqbara is one of the most recognisable monuments of Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar. Its architecture reflects the influence of Mughal design and makes it an important part of the city's historical landscape.

📜 HISTORICAL RECORD

The True Story

The monument was commissioned in the 17th century and is associated with Dilras Banu Begum, the wife of Mughal prince Azam Shah. Its design has often led visitors to compare it visually with the Taj Mahal.

Heritage Highlight

The monument is an important example of Mughal architectural influence in the Deccan.

Stories & Legends

Heritage places often carry stories passed through generations. Local traditions and visitor folklore add another layer to how people experience historic monuments.
